Foghorn Leghorn was raised on a small farm in the southeastern United States, where he developed an early interest in acoustics, animal behavior, and the physics of unusually elaborate practical jokes. His frequent observations of barnyard dynamics inspired a lasting curiosity about motion, impact, and the communication of scientific ideas at considerable volume.

He earned a Bachelor of Science in Physics from the University of Southern Barnyard Studies and later completed a Master of Science in Applied Animation at Acme State University. During his graduate studies, he investigated collision resilience, unstable mechanical systems, and the interpretation of technicolor observational data.

Foghorn entered the doctoral program in Physics at The University of Texas Rio Grande Valley, where he conducted research under the supervision of Dr.\ Daffy Duck. His dissertation examined animated physical systems using
frame-based measurements, statistical modeling, and archival data. His work has been presented at meetings of the American Association for Cartoon Physics and published in the \textit{Journal of Animated Physics}.

His research interests include experimental mechanics, animated dynamics, scientific communication, and the persistent misuse of Acme-manufactured equipment. Following completion of the doctorate, he plans to continue research and teaching while encouraging future students to think carefully, test assumptions, and listen when someone says, ``I say, I say.''
