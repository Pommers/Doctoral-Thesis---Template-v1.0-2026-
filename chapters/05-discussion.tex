\section{Overview: How Simulation has Evolved}

Previous work has explored the interpretation of simluated observations
\citep{scholar2022methods, mentor2023methods}. In particular,
\citet{example2024analysis} examined the consequences of repeated simulation
failure, while related reality feedback mechnisms were discussed by
\citet{researcher2025observations}.

The analysis presented here follows the general framework of
\citet{researcher2026observations} and was performed using the fictitious
\textsc{AcmeLab} software \citep{taylor2021dissertation}.
