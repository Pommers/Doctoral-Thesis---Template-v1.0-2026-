\section{Overview: How Technicolor Animation has Evolved}

Previous work has explored the interpretation of animated observations
\citep{duck2024technicolor,bunny2022methods}. In particular,
\citet{coyote2025road} examined the consequences of repeated experimental
failure, while related dynamical effects were discussed by
\citet{sam2023dynamite}.

The analysis presented here follows the general framework of
\citet{leghorn2026animation} and was performed using the fictitious
\textsc{AcmeLab} software \citep{acme2026software}.
